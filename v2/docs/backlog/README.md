# Backlog — what is open, and what is merely wanted

| document | what it is |
|---|---|
| [TODO.md](TODO.md) | **the plan we are executing** — work somebody has decided to do, in the order we intend to do it |
| [KNOWN-ISSUES.md](KNOWN-ISSUES.md) | defects that are real and unfixed, and that nobody has scheduled. Deliberately not in TODO: an open list that keeps things nobody intends to do stops being a plan |
| [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md) | the wider backlog: captured, not designed, each with a rough size and a "decide first" flag |

What was done, and what a measurement settled, is not here: history is in
[../records/PROGRESS.md](../records/PROGRESS.md) and the settled decisions are in
[../spec/REVIEW.md](../spec/REVIEW.md) — "Decisions not to change".

Both are lists rather than [plans](../plans/README.md): a plan is what an idea becomes once it has a
design and a verdict.
