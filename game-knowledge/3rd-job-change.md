# 3rd job change (3차 전직)

**Source:** [sealonline.co.kr — Guide, Quest → Job change → 3rd job change](https://www.sealonline.co.kr/Guide/Info?seq=678#)
**Server:** Korean (kr) · **Read:** 2026-09-17 · **Status:** complete as published

The page is short and is reproduced in full. The Korean is the original; the translation follows it
rather than replacing it, so a term can be checked against the source when the English is ambiguous.

## Original

> **3차 전직**
>
> 1. 300레벨 및 2차 전직을 완료한 상태에서 330년 맵에 있는 '[NPC] 시련의 인도자'와 대화를 진행하면
>    3차 전직 시험에 도전할 수 있습니다.
>    ※ 330년 맵은 엘림 마을 → 수련의 탑 → 이상한 워프를 통해 입장이 가능합니다.
>    ※ 330년 맵으로 이동이 불가능하다면 D.엔드와 선행 대사를 통해 플래그를 습득해야합니다.
> 2. 이동된 시험장에서 시험을 진행합니다.
> 3. [한계 돌파의 증표]를 획득한 뒤 '[NPC] 시련의 인도자'과 대화하여 [한계 돌파의 증표]를 건네주면
>    3차 전직이 완료됩니다.

## Translation

**3rd job change**

1. **At level 300, with the 2nd job change already complete**, talk to **`[NPC] 시련의 인도자`**
   ("Guide of Trials") on the **330-year map** to attempt the 3rd job change trial.
   - The 330-year map is reached through **Elim Village → Tower of Training → the strange warp**.
   - If you cannot travel to the 330-year map, you must first acquire the flag by talking to
     **D.End** and through the prerequisite dialogue.
2. Complete the trial in the trial grounds you are moved to.
3. Obtain the **`[한계 돌파의 증표]`** ("Proof of Breaking Limits"), then talk to
   **`[NPC] 시련의 인도자`** again and hand it over — that completes the 3rd job change.

## What is not on the page

Worth recording so nobody assumes it was missed: the page does **not** say what the trial consists of,
what level the trial grounds require, whether the trial can fail, or whether the prerequisite dialogue
through D.End is a quest or a one-off flag. It is a route description, not a walkthrough.

It also says nothing about the **Taiwanese** service, which is the one the tool is calibrated against.
The level requirement (300) and the NPC names are the parts most likely to differ, and both would need
confirming against the TW client before anything is built on them.
